# Baby Shower Planning Kit — Product Plan & Etsy Listing

## What this is
A 14-page printable PDF: cover, planning timeline (8 weeks out → day of), budget tracker,
guest list, gift & thank-you tracker, venue/vendor checklist, menu planner, theme & decor
page, shopping list, games & activities planner, day-of timeline, registry essentials
reference, key contacts, and a notes/closing page. Same pastel design system as the other
two Little Milestone Co. products.

## Positioning
Different buyer than the other two listings — this is bought by a **host** (friend,
sister, mother), not the expecting parent. Wider funnel, high search volume category.
Cross-link from this listing back to the planner/memory book for the parent-to-be gift
angle ("planning the shower? here's what to get them for after").

## Files
- `build_kit.py` — generates `shower_kit.html` (14 `.page` divs) and renders it to
  `Baby-Shower-Planning-Kit.pdf` via Playwright. Same `PASTEL` dict / page() pattern as
  `build_book.py` in the memory book folder — edit content there if you want more products
  in this style later.
- `Baby-Shower-Planning-Kit.pdf` — upload this directly as the Etsy digital file.
- `listing-images/hero.png`, `listing-images/pages-preview.png` — 2000x2000 listing photos.
